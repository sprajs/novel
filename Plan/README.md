# Post-freeze human ideas

The active programme starts with an auditable corpus of human research ideas
and observational measurement of a frozen model. **No candidate is admitted and
no scientific model-performance result is reported.**

The original plan is [archived with byte hashes](archive/pre-pr4/provenance.json).
Its corrected CUSP analysis and useful causal controls remain available. The
[ground-truth audit](../docs/ground_truth/README.md) remains a distinct, unchanged
source of exploratory claims, exact development fixtures and exclusions.

No paid workloads, large models, full copyrighted papers, private data or sealed
confirmation items are included. Existing CPU/GPU evidence retains its stated
limitations; the separate inference implementation must validate its own path.
