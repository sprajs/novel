# Machine discovery research guide

Novel aims to understand and improve the generation of useful scientific ideas.
The programme moves from defensible evaluation to mechanistic explanation,
controlled improvements and prospective discovery. Its first study builds an
auditable corpus of human contributions and measures historical reconstruction
by fixed models. **No candidate is admitted and no scientific model-performance
result is reported.**

The [evidence collection](../docs/ground_truth/README.md) supplies exploratory
claims, exact development fixtures and documented exclusions. Its synthetic
tasks and algebraic identities calibrate the instruments; corpus admission
requires independent review of the actual scientific contribution.

No paid workloads, large models, full copyrighted papers, private data or sealed
confirmation items are included. Existing CPU/GPU evidence retains its stated
limitations; the separate inference implementation must validate its own path.
