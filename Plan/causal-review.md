# Gates for causal studies

The active [HC-1 protocol](hidden-context-protocol.md) first establishes synthetic
behavioural transfer. Its later model interventions require a competent baseline,
held-out task groups, selective controls, no-op/capture parity and a separate
frozen hypothesis. They do not require admission of a historical scientific claim.

The requirements below govern interventions claiming to improve **scientific
idea generation**. That later track needs an admitted claim, reliable semantic
outcomes and independent scientific judgment. A synthetic behavioural effect
cannot satisfy those requirements by itself.

Read-only activations, likelihood contrasts and expressed reasoning can nominate
a mechanistic hypothesis. They cannot by themselves show that an internal state
caused discovery, that a concept is absent, or that a model left its training
distribution. Weights remain fixed in the observation phase; a raw parameter or
activation norm is not a universal distance from a model to an idea.

A scientific-discovery causal follow-up requires an admitted claim, a reliable semantic outcome, a
capable baseline and new held-out events. State a selective prediction: a proposed
intervention should improve valid solutions to the relevant mechanism while
preserving unrelated competence and abstention on insufficient information.
Choose sites, amplitudes and donors on development only; do not patch merely
because a retrospective heatmap looked interesting.

Required controls:

- Independent free-generation target and any-valid-solution outcomes, with blinded
  human adjudication; teacher-forced score changes remain secondary.
- Identity/capture-only parity, earlier-prefix invariance, token-span alignment,
  cache semantics and numerical tolerances measured on the actual backend.
- Different-answer, separate-event donors; answer-only, wrong/irrelevant, generic
  task, random-norm and sign-reversed controls; no hidden test labels in extraction.
- Both disruption and restoration, alternate sites and rescue where justified.
  A successful patch may bypass the model's ordinary route; a null ablation may
  reflect redundancy. Neither has a universal interpretation.
- Equal total compute and equal selection information for ordinary sampling,
  prompt variation, clean refinement and the proposed intervention. Charge
  development, prefill, scoring and evaluator calls, not only returned tokens.

Perturb-and-recover remains a specific later question: does a temporary internal
perturbation increase valid semantic yield per declared budget? Separate retained
noisy KV history, clean re-prefill of the realized text, and fresh clean refinement.
Turning off noise does not undo generated tokens or retained cache entries. Each
recovery policy needs a matched no-noise comparison and its own cost accounting.
Do not combine noisy generation plus oracle selection and call the gain an effect
of noise alone.

Primary methodological references are [patching best practices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.16042),
[patching interpretation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.15255) and
[faithfulness interventions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.13702).
Audit exact source versions and current evidence before claiming a new method.
These are study requirements; the repository's bounded activation mechanics
do not establish a causal discovery result.
