# Admission protocol for human ideas disclosed after a frozen model existed

Prepared 11 October 2026. This is a proposed corpus procedure and AI-assisted public-source research. **No case is admitted, no independent human review has occurred, and no historical model has been run.** The public [casebook](candidate-casebook.md) is development material. It contains claim summaries and draft problems, not a sealed confirmation set.

The corpus unit is an independently meaningful human research contribution paired with one exact historical model snapshot and one fixed input packet. A publication date or an old algebraic identity is insufficient. The intended result is historical reconstruction of a contribution unavailable in that snapshot's verified public history; it would not establish novelty to science today. The existing [ground-truth packets](../docs/ground_truth/packets/README.md) remain useful composition/calibration material. Their equation checkers do not admit the later research contributions.

## Scope and admission states

Prioritize computer science, physics, engineering, and finance. Select problems where domain knowledge is structured enough to state assumptions and judge proposals, but correctness and research value require substantive human assessment. Equations, simulation, small examples, counterexamples, and program checks can support that judgment. They must not reduce the target to an older mechanically checked identity or silently certify an empirical claim.

`exploratory` means a lead with unresolved admission gates. `rejected` means a recorded disqualification for this scoped claim/model/input pairing. An abstract `admitted` status would require all mandatory gates, independent human sign-offs, and a frozen record; the lifecycle distinguishes `admitted_development` from `admitted_confirmation`. Neither admitted state is populated here. `withdrawn` preserves a previously admitted record invalidated by new evidence. Do not erase rejected or withdrawn cases, backdate an admission, or reuse their historical result under a revised claim ID.

Treat human origin as evidence to collect. A human author list establishes attribution, not an audit that a particular idea was human-generated or that AI contributed nothing. Record named authors, contributions/acknowledgments where available, any disclosed machine assistance, and unresolved origin questions. A result explicitly generated by AlphaDev, AlphaEvolve, FunSearch, or another search system is not automatically a human-idea positive example. Human framing or subsequent validation does not change the provenance of the selected invention.

## Exact contribution before temporal selection

The domain reviewer first states the target in one or two falsifiable sentences, its assumptions, its best known precursor, and the residual contribution beyond that precursor. Classify the contribution as method/architecture, physical mechanism, theorem/proof strategy, experimental design, economic model, or measured empirical result. Write explicit exclusions. A novel method may use classical mathematics; solving that mathematics alone earns prerequisite credit. A new measurement is not a deductive target unless the required observations are supplied and the remaining inference is well posed.

Use three independent output questions:

1. Is the submitted proposal scientifically valid under the declared assumptions?
2. Does it recover the targeted contribution, up to the preregistered substantive equivalence relation?
3. If it is another valid solution, does it add a defensible contribution beyond the pre-disclosure baseline?

Retain separate labels for target recovery, valid alternative, familiar earlier solution, and unresolved proposal. Failure to match the reference paper need not mean failure to solve the problem. Conversely, a correct familiar method is not recovery of the targeted later contribution. The equivalence relation concerns mechanism, objective, proof ingredients, constraints, and scope; wording or a matching final number is insufficient.

## Disclosure chronology and earlier-equivalent search

Maintain a timeline of individual evidence events. A journal publication, preprint submission, preprint announcement, conference submission, accepted-paper listing, thesis deposit, public talk, slide upload, code release, patent publication, and repository commit are different event kinds. Submission or manuscript receipt can be private. Git author/committer times do not prove when a repository was pushed publicly or changed from private to public. A later retrospective statement about an earlier manuscript is a lead requiring verification.

For every event record the exact claim location and whether it contains the contribution, only prerequisites, an equivalent result, or merely a title. Inspect original versions: a contribution added in v2 inherits v2's public disclosure boundary, while an earlier v1 contribution cannot be postponed to v2 or journal publication. Do not use a PDF's automatically rendered date as the archive event. Record a content/version discrepancy and inspect the originally deposited artifact before admission.

The search must include:

- Exact and earlier titles, all named authors, distinctive mechanisms, equivalent equations, and alternative terminology.
- Original preprint versions and references, citation predecessors, proceedings and workshop abstracts, OpenReview or conference pages where applicable.
- Author/institution pages, public theses and technical reports, talks/slides/videos, official code releases and public code reviews, patent publication records where relevant.
- Closely related non-ML fields: control, information theory, statistical mechanics, optimization, microstructure, or mechanical design can contain the same contribution under different terminology.

Log each query, searched service, date, inspected result/version, claim comparison, and access failure. Search-engine date filters are leads; confirm dates on primary records. A query with no result is not proof of no predecessor. The temporal reviewer uses the earliest **verified claim-bearing or equivalent public channel found**, states the unresolved channels, and makes a bounded admission judgment. No finite search certifies the universal absence of prior art.

If an earlier equivalent is found, either reject the later novelty boundary or define a distinct residual contribution and restart its review. Do not merely rename the old mechanism. If an unverified but plausible earlier disclosure could predate the chosen model, chronology remains unresolved. An author-stated initial-version date before model release has exactly this consequence until its public visibility and claim content are resolved.

## Immutable model and input provenance

Use the [model audit](../docs/ground_truth/model_audit.md) and [file-level evidence](../data/ground_truth/model_audit.json) as existing leads. The historical pin includes repository ID, full revision, all weight object identities, model configuration, tokenizer files, chat template bytes, generation configuration, and any learned adapters, merged components, retrieval indexes, or post-training. Never resolve `main`, a moving step branch, or a family name at run time.

The timing condition is actual immutable snapshot availability before the earliest supported disclosure of the exact target contribution. Audit the complete information-bearing condition, not only base pretraining. Do not infer training cutoff from release, model failure, lack of a named entity, or a publisher's approximate knowledge date. Store an advertised cutoff separately with its source, scope, precision, and uncertainty. Missing cutoff evidence remains null/unknown.

For Pythia's April 2023 replacement suite, retain the original pinned `.bin` objects and co-located tokenizer. Later safetensors, moving `step143000`, deduplicated variants, v0, or another seed require distinct records and identity checks. For April Qwen3, retain the original April tokenizer/template, including the chosen thinking mode; July revisions and May template changes are distinct conditions even when weight identities match. Existing publisher release statements and histories corroborate these pins but leave independent historical public-visibility evidence unresolved.

Every prompt, system instruction, donor, bridge, tool result, retrieval source, and scaffold needs its own input provenance. A later evaluator can author neutral wording, but record that authorship date and the hindsight used to select the problem. Separately distinguish old facts from post-disclosure pair selection, bibliography selection, bridge construction, target-derived terminology, and target-derived experimental observations. These are assistance, not weight training, and can carry the solution.

Represent uncertain dates as intervals with source precision. Where actual availability/disclosure bounds are established, the conservative inequality is `latest_model_availability < earliest_target_disclosure`. Overlap or unknown ordering stays unresolved; same-day day-only evidence does not pass. This is an operational comparison of supported events, not proof that undisclosed private copies or unobserved public channels never existed.

## Independent human adjudication

Admission needs two independent recorded human assessments: a domain expert for contribution scope, prior-art novelty, problem sufficiency, scientific validity criteria, and alternative solutions; and a provenance reviewer for target disclosure, equivalent antecedents, exact model/interface availability, and all visible inputs. Reviewers disclose conflicts and relevant expertise. A researcher may fill either role, but one person must not supply both independent signatures. Where rights are unclear, a competent rights review is an additional unresolved gate.

Both reviewers work before model results and before knowing which intervention is favored. The domain reviewer writes a proposed solution/rationale and serious competing routes without reading future model outputs. The temporal reviewer independently follows primary sources rather than signing a generated summary. They record disagreements separately. A third appropriately qualified human resolves material disagreements; unresolved disagreement blocks admission. AI agents can find sources, propose comparisons, and run checks, but AI agreement is never recorded as independent human expert review.

Before scoring an output, blind its model, condition, seed, and source of assistance. Domain judges first assess validity from the supplied assumptions, then use the reference and precursor dossier to assess target equivalence and originality. For proposals needing new empirical work, distinguish plausible/testable from validated. At least two independent domain judgments are desirable for outcome scoring; report agreement, abstentions, and third-review adjudications. Scoring procedures and human workload are proposed; no reviewer has been recruited in this work.

## Admission gates and record requirements

Gate values are `passed`, `failed`, `unresolved`, or `not_assessed`. A numerical priority score cannot override a gate. All mandatory gates must pass before admission; pending reviewers, unavailable sources, speculative model timing, or unresolved rights cannot be silently treated as passes.

| Gate key | Minimum evidence before a pass |
| --- | --- |
| `human_origin` | Attributed contribution and reviewed origin evidence; explicit handling of disclosed AI/search assistance |
| `contribution_novelty` | Scoped residual contribution, best precursor, semantic-equivalence audit, human domain verdict |
| `disclosure_chronology` | Claim-bearing original-version timeline, bounded channel/search log, resolved date conflicts |
| `model_snapshot_chronology` | Exact complete snapshot identities and evidence it existed publicly before target disclosure |
| `input_provenance` | Every visible component and learned/tool component dated, classified, and linked to its evidence |
| `rights_and_publication` | Source/code/data/model rights distinguished; permitted public subset specified |
| `problem_well_posedness` | Stated assumptions, sufficient available information, feasible answer scope, empirical limits |
| `domain_judgment` | Signed independent human scope/novelty/validity/alternative review bound to artifact digests |
| `temporal_judgment` | Signed independent human chronology/provenance review bound to the same frozen artifacts |
| `leakage_review` | Visible projection examined for names, equations, numbers, vocabulary, references, and scaffolds that reveal the target |
| `preregistration_and_split` | Selection/rubric/assistance frozen before outcomes; event lineage, custody, and exclusions recorded |

The later machine schema should require these groups rather than compress them into `clean: true`:

| Record group | Required fields and semantics |
| --- | --- |
| Identity/state | Stable `case_id`, immutable record revision, `status`, `split`, domain, contribution kind, authors, origin evidence, selection date/reason, exclusion/withdrawal reason |
| Contribution | Target claim, assumptions, exclusions, precursor claim, residual contribution, equivalence relation, empirical claims beyond the assay |
| Source event | Source ID, primary URL, title/authors, upstream revision/version, claim location, event kind/date/precision/timezone, public-visibility evidence, accessed date, access outcome, limitations; `content_sha256` null unless bytes were actually acquired and hashed |
| Chronology | All disclosure events, earliest verified event ID, uncertainty interval, pending channels, earlier-equivalent results, search log, conflicts, reviewer verdict; submission and public release stored separately |
| Model pair | Repository, full immutable revision, weight and tokenizer/config/template file identities, post-training/adapters, complete public-availability evidence, source of each identity, advertised cutoff with scope or null, unresolved pin/visibility issues |
| Run interface | Exact tokenizer/template revision, rendered prompt digest when available, template arguments/thinking mode, generation configuration, software/precision/quantization, retrieval/tools and their learned components; proposed settings must not masquerade as executed settings |
| Visible input | Neutral problem text or custody reference, approved fields, pre-disclosure premises and sources, observations, allowed tools, authored date, hindsight-selection record, assistance classification; target solution and metadata excluded from the projection |
| Judgment | Independent validity, target equivalence, valid alternative, earlier-method equivalence, originality, usefulness, and feasibility criteria; adjudication guide, accepted alternatives, tempting wrong routes, uncertainty/abstention policy, supporting check limits |
| Reviews/gates | Explicit gate map; append-only reviewer role/identity/type, expertise/conflicts, date, reviewed hashes, independent verdict, dissent, resolution and reviewer signature/reference |
| Split/custody | Whole event/mechanism lineage ID, development/confirmation assignment, public exposure status, custodian, sealed artifact references/digests, selection chronology; no path from a worked public event into confirmation |
| Rights | Distinct paper/code/data/model license URLs and versions, terms/status, retained third-party content, publication permissions and public/private boundary |

Use null plus an explicit uncertainty/status explanation for unmeasured facts; empty arrays must not imply a completed clean search or completed review. A validator can enforce record structure, immutable-looking revision strings, and forbidden admission states in examples. It cannot validate scientific novelty, prove historical public visibility, substitute for human judgment, or certify source licenses.

## Draft problem construction, controls, and leakage

Start from a pre-disclosure problem statement with genuine constraints. Ask for a method/mechanism/design and a validation plan, not the future paper title, its signature equation, or its measured score. A model that is asked to do something not derivable from the visible information can produce a hypothesis; it cannot be credited with validating that hypothesis. Publish a sufficiency analysis and distinguish missing information from failed generation.

The evaluator holds the full source dossier, target rubric, precursor comparison, and bridge explanation. Visible prompts exclude candidate IDs, future names/citations, dates that identify a paper, signature performance numbers, target-derived implementation fragments, and hidden expected outputs. Human reviewers assess substantive leakage; token/string scans support them. A minimal bridge must be characterized as assistance and cannot be assumed harmless merely because it lacks the final formula. Target-directed representation choices may reveal the central idea.

Use the condition names in [the measurement protocol](measurement-protocol.md): `P0 historical_problem`, `P1 supplied_prerequisites`, `P2 structural_hint`, and `P3 supplied_construction`. P0 requires an independently justified pre-disclosure question and constraints. An anonymized rewrite of a future abstract is not automatically P0: it may reveal the novelty-defining representation, model assumptions or combination. The casebook drafts are hindsight-framed proposals with no certified P0 provenance. P1 adds curator-selected old prerequisites and records that selection as assistance; P2 adds the bridge/strategy. Only the paired P1-versus-P2 bridge comparison keeps those explicit prerequisites identical. P0-versus-P1 instead measures added knowledge/selection assistance. Matched irrelevant guidance and answer-bearing leakage diagnostics have separate labels. Treat a selected pair of old inspirations as hindsight assistance unless selection was independently made before target disclosure. A current retrieval tool or donor derived from a later paper changes the historical condition and requires a separate claim.

Feasibility must preserve the contribution. The proposed response cap is at most 2,048 tokens including reasoning and final answer, further limited by the model's full context window. Before admission, a human expert must show that a meaningful scoped idea, its critical justification, and failure conditions can fit the chosen packet/budget. A generic outline of a long theorem is not proof recovery, and a shortened prompt that supplies the hard step is direct assistance. If a claim needs a long proof, unavailable empirical evidence, or more context than the historical checkpoint provides, defer or reject the pairing rather than substitute an old elementary consequence. No domain quota requires filling the corpus.

Select events for scientific relevance, novelty isolation, chronological evidence, breadth, and feasible adjudication before seeing target-model successes or failures. Prerequisite and instruction-following pilots can guide feasibility, but their failures must not define which events count as novel. Record all pilot exclusions prospectively. Sample counts and accepted-output rates belong in [the measurement protocol](measurement-protocol.md); six worked cases do not supply six independent confirmation events, and repeated samples are not additional events.

## Source rights and public/held-out boundaries

Link primary materials and write original short summaries. Do not upload full copyrighted papers, restricted benchmark rows, author figures, or paid source data. The license on a paper, code repository, model, benchmark, or underlying dataset governs that artifact; none automatically clears the others. An arXiv nonexclusive distribution license is not a general redistribution license. Creative Commons paper permissions still require the correct attribution and conditions; [CC BY](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) and [CC BY-NC-SA](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/) differ materially. These links support the source distinctions, not a completed legal determination for the corpus.

Public development can contain complete target summaries, review instructions, and original problem drafts. Their exposure makes them permanently ineligible as sealed confirmation. Confirmation events and their full semantic families must be selected and held by a separate custodian; publish only protocol and commitments before unsealing. Hiding a paper title while exposing its distinctive solution does not preserve holdout. Later publication of admitted confirmation dossiers follows the preregistered unsealing procedure and source rights; until then, the repository should not contain those dossiers or answer-bearing bridge variants.

## Current disposition

The [six worked leads](candidate-casebook.md) all remain `exploratory` and `development`. The earlier CUSP correction is retained: its detailed auditor exempts matching methods and numerical predictions; an output matching a future result alone is not contamination. Conversely, a pass or named-entity judgment does not prove training provenance. [The existing benchmark audit](../docs/ground_truth/benchmark_audit.md) documents this narrower conclusion and the inspected source versions.

The next concrete work is human scope/novelty review, channel-level chronology, complete historical pin/public-visibility evidence, neutral-input review, and a prospectively fixed outcome rubric. No corpus fill quota can replace these gates. This document neither promotes existing equation fixtures nor authorizes downloading models, running experiments, purchasing compute, contacting authors, or publishing restricted content.
